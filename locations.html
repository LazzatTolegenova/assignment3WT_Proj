Frequently asked questions

Message support in the app no later than 2 hours before your session.

Yes, if the chosen studio has free spots — you can do this right in the app.

Is booking included in my membership?

Yes, booking a session is included in every active Healthy plan.

Can I train near my home?

Yes — use the city and activity filters to find the closest studio.
